The Writer

They call him NM — spelled ENEM, pronounced with a Philly accent. A citizen of Philadelphia, “the virtual place of freedom.” Thirty years deep, a star of the groundbreaking documentary INFAMY, and — as VANDAL TO VANGOGH proved — a veteran emcee with the same razor control on the mic as on the wall.

Before the name locked in, it moved:

    KING DREAM · R.I.P.

    The biggest influence. “He always told me to push my own style. He believed in me. I hope the way I do graff as a lifestyle is his impact on me. Dream never really stopped in my eyes.”

    DREAM TDKFOREVER

    BLENDS, NOT COLLABS

    “I don’t call em collabs, I call em blends — I like the idea of mixing minds on any project. I also don’t call mural art street art. I’m a street artist… graffiti writers are the only people I believe can actually be a street artist. It’s not a diss, it’s a distinction.”

    BLACKBOOK · ink on paper

    The same hand that cuts walls cuts characters. Pages like this are why the “VANDAL TO VANGOGH” artwork came from the man himself.
